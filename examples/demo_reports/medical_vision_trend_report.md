## Generated Report

Medical AI vision research is moving toward multimodal systems that combine images, text, temporal context, and action signals. For surgical scenarios, robust perception must handle occlusion and viewpoint shifts.

Future work should evaluate multimodal alignment quality, test generalization across datasets, and connect visual understanding with planning-oriented models.
